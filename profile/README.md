PROPSTAT is a European real estate platform that aggregates diverse public and private data to provide transparency and actionable market insights to professionals, homebuyers, and policymakers. The platform operates by compiling extensive data sources, transforming them into analytics, and utilizing this information for predictive market modeling. Explore the platform at [propstat.org](https://propstat.org).
